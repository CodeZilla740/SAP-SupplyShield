# SupplyShield — 5-Minute Demo Script

Preparation: `npm run build && npm start`, open http://localhost:4004.
Reset before the demo with `curl -X POST localhost:4004/api/admin/reseed`.

## 0:00 — Overview (30 s)

"SupplyShield is a control tower for hospital medicine supply chains. This is
a live network: 124 shipments in transit, 3 active disruptions, 4 hospitals at
risk. Everything you see is served from the backend database — on the footer
you can see which engine is active: SAP HANA Cloud, or the clearly-labelled
local fallback."

Point at the agent ribbon: five agents, run SS-00942.

## 0:30 — Disruption Monitor (45 s)

"Cyclone Varun has closed Chennai Port for 48 hours — a *verified* disruption.
Note the other two events are marked UNVERIFIED: our weather integration can
raise advisory alerts, but an alert is never treated as proof a port is
closed."

Click **Run impact assessment**. "This triggers the five-agent pipeline
against the live database."

## 1:15 — Shipment Intelligence (30 s)

Open SS-2048. "The delayed insulin shipment for St. Martha Hospital.
Temperature telemetry 3.8–5.2 °C, still inside the 2–8 °C band — the product
is fine, the *timing* is the problem."

## 1:45 — Inventory & Stock Risk (45 s)

"St. Martha has 120 vials, demand is 100 per day — 28.8 hours of cover against
a 48-hour delay. The backend computes an 80-vial shortfall and a 240-vial
transfer (two full cases plus a one-day buffer). Hyderabad is shown as a donor
candidate but discarded: it only has 150 vials above its own safety stock."

## 2:30 — AI Recovery Planning (60 s)

"Three scenarios, all computed — not hardcoded:

- **Option A, air rerouting**: fastest compliant-looking option at 8 hours,
  but the Compliance agent rejects it — the carrier's cold-chain certification
  is not verified. Deterministic rule R1, no AI override.
- **Option B, Bengaluru stock transfer**: 6 hours, $1,100, passes all rules —
  recommended with a computed score of 85/100.
- **Option C, wait**: stockout roughly 19 hours before replenishment."

## 3:30 — Cold-Chain Compliance (30 s)

"The rules are pharmaceutical governance, not suggestions: temperature band,
carrier certification, donor safety stock, batch validity. 98.7% network
compliance, live telemetry, batch record valid through September 2028."

## 4:00 — Approval Centre (45 s)

"The AI recommends; a human decides. The planner reviews the explanation —
generated by SAP Generative AI Hub when configured, otherwise a clearly
labelled rule-based template — adds a comment, and approves."

Click **Approve recovery plan**.

"What you can't see: the server re-validated rules R1–R3 against live data at
the moment of the click. If the stock situation had changed, the approval
would be blocked with an error — the AI cannot push a non-compliant plan
through."

## 4:45 — Execution & Audit Trail (15 s)

"The decision is now part of a hash-chained, tamper-evident audit log — every
agent step and the human decision share one correlation ID, SS-00942-F7A. The
execution reference TR-SIM-xxxxx is explicitly labelled SIMULATED: no real
S/4HANA transaction is posted by this prototype."

## Closing line

"Sense, analyse, comply, decide — with deterministic rules the AI can explain
but never override, and a complete audit trail for every action."
